# Portland Public Awareness Campaign: Community Outreach Plan

## Executive Summary
This document outlines the operational plan for a multi-neighborhood public awareness campaign in Portland, Oregon. Outreach activities are distributed across three strategic high-traffic hubs:
1. **Downtown Portland** (focused around Pioneer Courthouse Square)
2. **The Pearl District** (centered around Powell's City of Books)
3. **Southeast Portland** (along the SE Hawthorne Boulevard corridor)

## 4. Logistics & Contingency Checklist
1. **Permits:** Confirm sidewalk / street-use tabling permits with Portland Bureau of Transportation (PBOT) and Portland Parks & Recreation (for Pioneer Courthouse Square).
2. **Indoor Backup Protocols:** Maintain active room reservation holds at Multnomah County Central Library and SE Uplift in the event of unexpected local conditions or indoor presentation requirements.
3. **Hydration & Sun Protection:** Given daytime highs reaching 81°F–84°F on Thursday and Sunday, provide water stations and pop-up shade tents for volunteer teams.
4. **Transit Access:** All three target areas are heavily served by TriMet (Downtown MAX lines, Portland Streetcar in Pearl, TriMet Bus Lines 14 and 15 in Hawthorne).
