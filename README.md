# Portland Community Outreach Campaign

Welcome to the **Portland Public Awareness & Community Outreach Campaign** repository. This project coordinates public education and engagement across three key geographic hubs in Portland, Oregon:

1. **Downtown Portland** (Pioneer Courthouse Square)
2. **The Pearl District** (Powell's City of Books corridor)
3. **Southeast Portland** (SE Hawthorne Boulevard corridor)

Detailed operational plans, venue directories, meteorological analysis, and recommended event dates are documented in [`campaign-plan.md`](./campaign-plan.md).

---

## Targeted Areas & Community Meeting Venues

### 1. Downtown Portland (Pioneer Courthouse Square Hub)
- **Multnomah County Central Library** (`801 SW 10th Ave`) – Historic flagship library with large civic meeting rooms (US Bank Room, Collins Gallery) and media spaces.
- **Pioneer Courthouse Square** (`701 SW 6th Ave`) – Portland's primary open-air civic plaza and amphitheater, directly served by all MAX light rail lines.
- **Portland City Hall** (`1220 SW 5th Ave`) – Public atrium and Council Chambers for official hearings, announcements, and press events.
- **Eliot Center at First Unitarian** (`1211 SW Main St`) – Versatile civic event venue featuring Buchan Reception Hall and Fuller Hall.

### 2. Pearl District (Powell's Books Hub)
- **Portland Center Stage at The Armory** (`128 NW 11th Ave`) – Historic armory located 1 block from Powell's Books, offering public lobbies and the Ellyn Bye Studio.
- **Jean Vollum Natural Capital Center / Ecotrust** (`721 NW 9th Ave`) – Community hub featuring the Billy Frank Jr. Conference Center, indoor atrium, and rooftop terrace.
- **Pacific Northwest College of Art (PNCA)** (`511 NW Broadway`) – Campus spaces including the Arlene and Harold Schnitzer Center and Mediatheque for community forums.

### 3. Southeast Portland (Hawthorne District Hub)
- **Multnomah County Belmont Library** (`1038 SE César E. Chávez Blvd`) – Public neighborhood library branch offering community meeting facilities.
- **Historic Belmont Firehouse** (`900 SE 35th Ave`) – Public safety learning center and event room operated in partnership with Portland Fire & Rescue.
- **SE Uplift Community Coalition** (`3534 SE Main St`) – Civic resource facility serving 20 Southeast Portland neighborhood associations with multi-use meeting rooms.
- **McMenamins Bagdad Theater & Backstage** (`3702 SE Hawthorne Blvd`) – Historic community cultural venue on Hawthorne Blvd.
